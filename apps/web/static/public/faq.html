Questions parents usually ask first

Plain answers, without marketing language.

Can a child add coins themselves?

No. Done sends a request to a parent; coins appear after approval.

Must rewards cost money?

No. Add a board game, walk, film choice, or time together.

What if there are several children?

Each child has a separate balance, task list, history, and limits.

Does a child need email?

No. A child account is connected to Telegram. Email is only for a parent who chooses it.

Can I invite another parent?

Yes, by email or Telegram. You do not need to share one password.

Does the bot replace the Mini App?

No. The bot handles quick actions; settings and long lists are easier in the Mini App.
